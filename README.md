# Adaptive SAT Learning Platform

An adaptive SAT preparation platform that uses student performance data, AI feedback, and tutor insights to create personalized study plans.

Instead of giving every student the same questions and schedule, the platform learns from their mistakes, identifies weak concepts, and recommends what to study next.

## The Goal

We are building for two audiences:

- **Students:** a clearer, more personalized, and less overwhelming way to prepare for the SAT.
- **Parents:** visible progress, accountability, and confidence that their child is studying effectively.

Tutors should spend less time diagnosing basic weaknesses and more time helping students with the concepts that actually need attention.

## Adaptive Learning Flow

```text
AI Coach
↓
Creates today's study plan
↓
