# Adaptive SAT Learning Platform

An AI-powered SAT prep platform that turns student mistakes into smarter study plans—helping students improve faster and tutors focus on the gaps that matter most.

## The Goal

We are building to bridge the gap between what parents and students want from SAT prep. Parents want measurable score improvement, accountability, and clear proof that their child is making progress. Students want studying to feel simpler, more personalized, less overwhelming, and more useful than working through generic question banks.

The platform adapts to each student’s performance, identifies why mistakes happen, and recommends exactly what to work on next.

## What Makes It Different

Traditional tutoring offers accountability but can be expensive and dependent on the individual tutor. Self-study platforms offer content at scale but often give generic plans and limited insight into why a student is struggling.

This platform combines both: adaptive practice for students, clear progress for parents, and tutor dashboards that highlight the highest-priority weaknesses, timing issues, and recurring mistake patterns.
